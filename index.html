Austin Martin, New Hampshire & Hawaii

How the LPNH disaffiliation, the Hawaii email and the fight over Region 1 fit together, 2021–2026

In November 2026 the South Carolina party passed a resolution opposing affiliate expulsion and a Hawaii disaffiliation, and its members argued over who started the disaffiliation talk and why Region 1 Representative Austin Martin was removed from LNC business. This page sets each claim against the LNC's minutes, its public email lists, its meeting recordings and the Judicial Committee's case record.

Chair Evan McMahon put the item on his own agenda and moved it after handing the gavel to the Vice-Chair. Keith Thompson moved the item earlier in the agenda and voted yes. Alfa Shaw moved twice to investigate first, lost both times, and voted no.

Its text is not on any list. A non-member sent the image to the LNC; Martin forwarded it to the public list. The Treasurer denied any move against Hawaii; another member called the email a trap set to catch a leaker. What was said is on the record; why it was sent is a claim.

F5

The Martin Conflict Began With The 2025 McArdle Report, Not With New Hampshire.

Martin called the board's 2025 investigative report on McArdle a fraud in public. The Chair's measures came next: a 24-hour takedown order and an email cut-off without a vote. Martin's harshest language followed, and from then on each side's step drew the next.

F6

What Colleagues Object To Is Conduct, Not His Votes, By Their Account.

Threatening and abusive language, leaking confidential material, disrupting meetings, and his treatment of counsel. He was censured twice, removed from two meetings, and suspended pending a November 21 trial. He calls himself a whistleblower and the discipline retaliation. The accounts on both sides are claims; the votes are on the record.

F7

The Judicial Committee Upheld The Disaffiliation 7-0, On A Record With Real Gaps.

No notice or investigation was owed, it held. At the hearing the LNC's Secretary agreed there was no formal warning and no urgency, and said he did not draft the motion. Only two of the seven Committee members were elected by delegates.
